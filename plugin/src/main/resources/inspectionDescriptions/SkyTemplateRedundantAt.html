Reports SkyTemplate @ modifiers whose effect is redundant or zero, and almost always a typo of the loop-scope form.

Two sub-cases:

  • Redundant @ on a non-loop variable. {var@} / {var@5} on a plain variable (no leading dot, not a reserved name) is silently ignored by parseNormalVar. The intended form is almost always {.var@N} or {_index@N}.
  • @0 on a loop-scope or reserved variable. {.var@0} / {_index@0} — the compiler treats @0 as @1 (one level up), so writing @0 reads as misleading "no offset" while actually meaning "parent loop".

Bare @ on a loop-scope variable ({.var@}) is intentional shorthand for {.var@1} and is NOT flagged.

Examples that trigger:

{var@}            {* @ on plain variable, ignored by compiler *}
{var@5}           {* same — typo of {.var@5} *}
{.var@0}          {* @0 reads as @1, confusing *}
{_index@0}        {* same — reserved-var case *}

Runs in *.sky files (this inspection) and in HTML host files (via the companion annotator). Honours the master Enable SkyTemplate support toggle.
